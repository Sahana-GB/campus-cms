\subsection{Profiling the enrollment queries}
We loaded 10{,}006 students, 43 courses, 50{,}040 enrollments and 70{,}301 grades, and timed the queries the
application runs most (30 repetitions each, same random sample before and after). Before adding any index,
\texttt{EXPLAIN QUERY PLAN} showed full table scans. The slowest query was \emph{Browse Courses}, at
146\,ms, because it counts the approved enrollments of every course and each count scanned the whole
\texttt{enrollments} table.

We added five indexes in \texttt{db/indexes.sql}: \texttt{enrollments(course\_id, status)},
\texttt{enrollments(status)}, \texttt{grades(student\_id)}, \texttt{assignments(course\_id)} and
\texttt{students(name COLLATE NOCASE)}. Table~\ref{tab:prof} shows the effect.

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\begin{tabular}{|l|r|r|r|}
\hline
Query & Before (ms) & After (ms) & Speed-up \\
\hline
Seats taken (one course)        & 3.122   & 0.059  & 53$\times$ \\
Admin card: count by status     & 3.442   & 0.274  & 12.6$\times$ \\
Admin table: one course, pending & 3.717  & 0.806  & 4.6$\times$ \\
Admin table: all pending        & 34.019  & 30.478 & 1.1$\times$ \\
Browse courses                  & 146.494 & 1.983  & 74$\times$ \\
CGPA of one student             & 2.512   & 0.025  & 100$\times$ \\
Report: enrollment counts       & 63.052  & 8.785  & 7.2$\times$ \\
Report: grade averages          & 98.840  & 99.633 & none \\
Student search by name prefix   & 1.017   & 0.175  & 5.8$\times$ \\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\caption{Average query time before and after adding indexes (SQLite, 10{,}006 students).}
\label{tab:prof}
\end{table}

Two queries did not improve. The grade-averages report has to read every published grade, so no index can
skip that work; it is an administrator report used rarely. The ``all pending'' list returns thousands of
rows, so its time goes into building and sorting the result. The name index only helps prefix searches;
a search for text in the middle of a name cannot use an index. Indexes cost a little extra time on inserts
and extra disk space, which is acceptable for a read-heavy system. We therefore added them only after
measuring, and \texttt{schema.sql} contains none.
